**Approved direction:** 17 October 2026 (America/Toronto).  
**Status:** Binding architecture and product decision, **not yet implemented, activated or production-qualified**. ## Outcome and selected services

Keep Justelle affordable for two members without compromising permanent, private, restorable records. Avoid repeatedly backing up immutable content from completed adventures.

- **Live application:** Firebase Auth, Firestore and member-protected Storage, as already selected. The live service does not run on a home computer.
- **Active disaster recovery:** Keep the existing separately administered, encrypted Backblaze B2 backup foundation, initially for short-term recovery of changing data. Use available free allowances where eligible; do not assume any cloud spending cap or free tier is guaranteed.
- **Permanent trip archives:** Use the owner's **existing Microsoft 365 Family OneDrive allowance**, with the owner's **Windows 11 mini PC (GMKtec)** as a local, scheduled transfer/verification client. Keep a **separate non-OneDrive-synchronised local copy** as a second recovery location; a further disconnected drive is a worthwhile optional improvement, not a launch prerequisite.
- **Existing capacity:** Owner reports ample spare storage across the family allowance. Verify the actual **per-account quota, folder permissions and available space** before implementation; do not infer that pooled family capacity can all be consumed by one login.
- **Additional storage subscription:** Target **C$0 extra for OneDrive** using the existing plan, with B2 likely inexpensive or free at early volumes; measure genuine use and retain cost controls. This is a cost objective, not an assertion of zero total cloud charges. Do not activate another paid storage tier simply for Justelle without checking actual need and approval.
- **Portability:** Archive content, manifests and recovery tools remain Justelle-defined, versioned and restorable without Firebase, OneDrive, Backblaze or any AI provider. The existing independent encrypted recovery code is the starting point.

Never publish account identities, device paths, cloud credentials, keys, real travel documents or backup files in the public repository or its logs. Operational secrets and personal recovery procedures belong in protected owner-controlled configuration.

## Adventure data lifecycle

1. **Planning and active trips:** Maintain one canonical working plan with immutable, attributable events; protect current records, original sources, booking confirmations and new media. Move towards **incremental/change-based backups and periodic independently restorable checkpoints**, not daily full recopying of an accumulating lifetime library. Content-identical immutable originals should be stored once and referenced by stable IDs and checksums.
2. **Completion:** Archiving is triggered by an authorised member marking the adventure complete, not by assuming that an intended date proves travel occurred. As an initial **adjustable design default**, offer archival after approximately 30 days for late receipts, corrections and lessons; members may archive sooner or defer.
3. **Sealed archive:** Produce an encrypted, versioned, provider-neutral adventure package with the full plan, historical revisions/Undo events, author and timestamp evidence, external booking anchors and statuses, original confirmations, images/screenshots/PDFs and relevant relationships. The archive manifest records stable IDs, file sizes/hashes, format/version, event boundary, and links to any later amendments. Preserve original bytes and provenance, not only AI-derived summaries. Extend the present image-oriented recovery format for documents and the newer planner records before claiming this works.
4. **Independent copies:** Have the Windows client retrieve the encrypted package through a narrowly scoped, authenticated route, verify its checksum, place one protected copy **outside** the OneDrive sync tree and synchronise another to OneDrive. Verify the remote copy and periodically perform a genuine clean restoration using the separately protected recovery key. OneDrive sync-status alone is not evidence of an independently restorable backup.
5. **Archive view:** Keep a small member-protected searchable index in Firebase (title, date certainty, places, cover/overview, lessons, archive IDs and availability) and back that index up. The full archived material remains accessible on demand, through authenticated retrieval and rehydration. Archiving must not remove useful reference access from the iPhone or expose direct public media links.
6. **After archival:** Unchanged sealed trip contents are **excluded from subsequent routine active snapshots** once the archive is verified and reachable through two recovery locations. Store later corrections/lessons as new attributable append-only amendments and protect those new records; do not silently rewrite or delete the sealed history. Full originals may remain in Firebase while inexpensive; moving/removing them is a separate, fully tested optimisation, not an automatic consequence of clicking Archive.

## Non-negotiable deletion and recovery rules

- **Fail closed:** Never remove a live source, B2 recovery generation or historical record because an upload merely started, a PC folder exists or OneDrive displays a sync icon. Verify complete packages, encrypted integrity, all relationships and **successful restore** from independent copies first. If the PC is asleep, offline, short of space, not signed in or OneDrive is unavailable, retain protected source data and retry later.
- OneDrive is a **synchronisation** service: deletes, overwrites, ransomware or account issues can propagate. The non-synchronised local copy must not be automatically deleted by OneDrive sync, and protected older generations must remain recoverable. A local copy on the same computer does not protect against theft or total site loss; ensure the OneDrive copy is independently accessible and consider an external drive for stronger resilience.
- Keep the **recovery encryption key separately** from stored archives, GitHub, logs, and OneDrive. Define recoverable owner custody and verify a clean-device restore before any source deletion. Losing the key makes the archive unrecoverable; encrypting alone does not prevent deletion.
- Respect B2 Object Lock or other retention commitments: do not assume an old backup can immediately be deleted after an archive transfer. Use bounded, measured retention for active recovery (illustrative defaults: seven daily recovery points and four weekly checkpoints), never a growing pile of full daily copies.
- Preserve canonical Justelle IDs, member-only access and the complete audit trail. A restored archive must not convert tentative plans into confirmed bookings or forge human endorsement. Moving a file must never imply an external reservation changed.
- Provide visible last-success/last-failure status and alerts for missed backups, capacity problems, integrity failures and restore drills. Avoid an invisible 'green' backup indicator based only on successful upload.

## Efficiency and operating principles

- Prefer **incremental metadata/event changes** and content-addressed (or checksum-deduplicated) immutable original media, with periodic verified baseline checkpoints. Separate archive text compression from already-compressed JPEG/HEIC/PDF binaries; never sacrifice original quality/provenance for savings.
- Do not load entire historical media collections, large PDFs, maps or AI contexts into the iPhone client; retrieve smaller views and originals when requested. Do not re-run paid research simply because an archived trip was opened. Cache dated findings and refresh only when justified.
- Keep the GMKtec **out of the production serving path**; do not expose new inbound Internet ports. It may use an outgoing Windows-scheduled task and a tightly scoped, revocable read capability for encrypted backup downloads; avoid broad Firebase administrator credentials or personal OneDrive credentials in a cloud function.
- Compare actual storage, transfer, Cloud Functions, verification, retention and Windows electricity costs with the existing B2-only alternative before introducing any further service. Prefer fewer providers and less operational friction when cash savings are negligible.

## Sequenced delivery and acceptance

**First safe release:** Preserve the existing encrypted B2 full-snapshot and independent-restore safeguards while the private application is being qualified. Extend backup/export/restore to every new adventure, booking, source, media and change-event type **before real trip records are admitted**. The present scheduled snapshot's **128 MiB limit** is a known constraint, not a scalable long-term archival design. Cloud-hosted fictional security, real-device and recovery acceptance remain required.

**Next low-cost improvement:** Implement versioned incremental/periodic-checkpoint recovery, retained manifests, completed-trip package sealing, Windows download/verify/sync, protected searchable archive index, amendment handling and authenticated retrieval. The Windows/OneDrive archive pipeline need not block first safe production use while the qualified B2 restore path is intact, but must be in place before accumulation or removal of substantial permanent trip originals.

**Prove with fictional data:** A completed trip with original images, PDF/source documents, bookings, history and late corrections can be archived, independently restored, searched, reopened and amended after Firebase and B2 are unavailable. Test corrupt/missing archives, wrong/lost keys, same-file retries, conflicting edits, late documents, synced deletion, account/quota failure, sleeping PC, offline Windows client, unverified OneDrive copy, denied outsider access, restoration on a clean computer and a complete disaster recovery of the active index. No deletion or routine-backup exclusion happens until its independent archives pass integrity and restore checks.

**Current implementation status:** Existing branch work creates full encrypted provider-neutral snapshots and a B2 transfer/restore verification flow with fictional-data tests; neither the new incremental architecture nor a GMKtec/OneDrive archive pipeline is built or live. This document authorises the engineering direction, not activation of cloud accounts, new paid services or claims of production backup verification.
